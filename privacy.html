Belge AI — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

Belge AI (“we”, “us”, or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Belge AI application (“App”).

By using Belge AI, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Process

PDF and Document Content

When you use features such as OCR, AI analysis, summarization, question answering, information extraction, rewriting, or PowerPoint conversion, the content of your document may be processed by our backend services to provide the requested functionality.

Document content is processed only as necessary to provide the requested feature.

OCR Data

If you scan a document using the camera or use OCR functionality, the resulting text may be processed by Belge AI's AI services when you request an AI-powered feature.

Anonymous Installation Identifier

Belge AI generates an anonymous installation identifier and stores it securely on your device using Apple's Keychain.

This identifier is used to:

  • Identify the application installation;
  • Track free AI operations;
  • Prevent resetting the free AI allowance by deleting and reinstalling the application;
  • Associate Premium subscription status with the installation.

The identifier does not directly identify you by name, email address, phone number, or physical address.

Subscription Information

If you purchase Belge AI Premium, Apple provides transaction information necessary to verify your subscription.

Belge AI's backend may store information such as:

  • Product identifier;
  • Transaction identifier;
  • Original transaction identifier;
  • Subscription expiration date;
  • Apple environment information;
  • Anonymous installation identifier;
  • Premium status.

Payment processing is handled by Apple through the App Store. Belge AI does not receive or store your credit card or other payment card information.

2. How We Use Information

Information processed by Belge AI is used to:

  • Provide PDF and document processing features;
  • Perform OCR;
  • Provide AI-powered analysis and summarization;
  • Answer questions about submitted documents;
  • Extract information from documents;
  • Rewrite or transform document content;
  • Convert documents to other formats;
  • Manage free AI usage limits;
  • Verify Premium subscriptions;
  • Maintain application security and reliability;
  • Diagnose technical problems and improve the service.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. AI Processing

Belge AI uses third-party AI infrastructure to provide certain AI-powered features.

When you request an AI feature, relevant document text may be securely transmitted to our backend infrastructure and processed by an AI service provider.

Belge AI currently uses Google's Gemini AI services for AI processing.

You should avoid submitting highly sensitive, confidential, or legally privileged information unless you are comfortable with it being processed for the requested feature.

4. Document Storage

Belge AI is primarily designed to process documents for the requested operation.

Unless explicitly stated otherwise within the App, Belge AI does not provide a permanent cloud document-storage service.

Documents may remain temporarily in memory or server processing infrastructure for the time required to complete a requested operation.

We do not use submitted documents to create advertising profiles.

5. AI Usage Limits

Free users receive a limited number of AI operations.

The current free allowance is five AI operations.

Belge AI maintains an anonymous usage count associated with the installation identifier.

Premium users are not subject to the free AI usage limit while their Premium subscription is active.

AI usage information is not intended to identify you personally.

6. Premium Subscriptions

Belge AI Premium subscriptions are purchased through Apple's App Store.

Apple handles payment processing and subscription billing.

Belge AI receives only the transaction information necessary to verify and manage the subscription.

For information about Apple's handling of payment and subscription data, please refer to Apple's applicable privacy policies and App Store terms.

7. Third-Party Services

Belge AI may use third-party services necessary to operate its features, including:

  • Apple / App Store — application distribution and subscription payments;
  • Google Gemini — AI processing;
  • Render — backend infrastructure;
  • Neon PostgreSQL — limited application usage and subscription information.

These services may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect information processed through Belge AI.

These measures include:

  • Secure HTTPS communication between the App and backend;
  • Secure storage of the anonymous installation identifier using Apple's Keychain;
  • Server-side storage of sensitive API credentials;
  • Server-side verification of Premium transactions;
  • Restricted access to backend systems.

However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

Belge AI is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

10. Your Choices

You may choose not to use AI-powered features.

You may also choose not to submit documents containing sensitive information.

Premium subscription management and cancellation are handled through your Apple App Store account.

11. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain subscription status, enforce usage limits, maintain security, or comply with legal obligations.

Anonymous AI usage records may be retained to prevent users from resetting their free allowance through application deletion and reinstallation.

12. International Data Processing

Depending on the infrastructure and services used to provide Belge AI, information may be processed on servers located outside your country.

By using AI-powered features, you acknowledge that information necessary to provide those features may be processed through third-party infrastructure.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

When changes are made, the updated version will be made available through the App or the location where this Privacy Policy is published.

Your continued use of Belge AI after an update constitutes acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.
